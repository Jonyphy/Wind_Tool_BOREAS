Yield · Probability

Exceedance probabilities

Turn an annual energy sum and a combined standard uncertainty into P50, P75, P90 — or any P-value you choose. Paste a column from Excel or enter a single value.

Formula: P(p) = E − z(p) · σ  ·  σ = (u% ⁄ 100) · E  ·  z = NORMSINV(p)

Inputs

One value, a whole column, or name + value pairs (e.g. copied straight from Excel).
Enter at least one value.
Build it from components (RSS)
Enter each uncertainty component in %. They are combined by root-sum-square and written above.

Results

P-values for the annual energy sum.
Enter energy values to see results.
