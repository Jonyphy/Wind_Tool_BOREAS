Site data · Wind direction

Wind rose & main wind direction

Enter a coordinate or load a shapefile. The tool pulls hourly wind from reanalysis models (ERA5, CERRA …) and draws one wind rose per model, so you can see the main wind direction and whether the models agree.

Location

Type a coordinate, click the map, or load a shapefile.
Read in your browser, nothing is uploaded. The centre of the bounding box is used. With a .prj the system is read from the file; without one, the coordinate system selected above is used.
The models give 10 m and 100 m. Other heights are estimated per hour from those two (power law, direction turned between them).
Choose a location and press “Draw wind roses”.
