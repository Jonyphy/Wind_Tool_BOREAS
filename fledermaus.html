Months in scope (season definition)

One month name per line, in any order, choosing however many months your local bat-active season covers. Names are matched case-insensitively against German or English spellings (e.g. Mai or May, Oktober or October). All 12 calendar months are supported — not just April–October — so a Southern-Hemisphere or shifted-season project (e.g. November–February) works the same way. Any month you don't list here is simply skipped everywhere else in the tool.

NZ time windows (Nachtzehntel)

One line per window: Month NZ start end (HH:MM). NZ0 is generated automatically: it starts 15% of the night length before NZ1 start and ends at NZ1 start. Each month can have a different number of windows (NZ1…NZ8, NZ1…NZ12, etc.) — just be consistent with the thresholds below.

Wind speed thresholds (m/s)

One line per month: Month NZ0 NZ1 … NZN — give one value per NZ window used by that month (N+1 values total, where N is that month's highest window index). Turbine stops when wind < threshold inside the window.

Classic scheme (reference)

Fixed night windows, single threshold. One line per month: Month start end.

Method notes

Weibull: loss = frequency-weighted E[P | v < threshold] from the sector wake-Weibull, multiplied by active hours. Assumes the annual wind distribution also holds during bat-active hours.

Time series: every timestep inside an NZ window with valid temperature is checked against the threshold; if wind < threshold, the power-curve value is added as loss. Captures the night/summer wind correlation. Optional per-turbine wind scale adjusts meteo wind to hub conditions.

Date parsing: the DateTime column supports ISO (YYYY-MM-DD), day-first (DD.MM.YYYY) and US month-first (MM/DD/YYYY) formats. Auto-detect scans a sample of rows and locks in whichever format is unambiguous from the data (a component >12 can only be a day); it warns you if the sample is fully ambiguous.

Result language: the interface can be switched between English and German, but all results, tables and exports are always written in German so reports stay consistent.

All computation runs locally in your browser.

How to use
1

Choose a method

Weibull reproduces the windPRO-style sector-statistics calculation. Time series evaluates every 10-min step of the meteo data (needs a wind speed column). Compare both shows them side by side.

2

Load meteo data

Select the meteo CSV (; or , separated), map the DateTime, temperature and wind columns, choose the date format (or leave it on Auto-detect), set the year range, then press Parse file. German decimal commas are handled automatically.

3

Add turbines

Per turbine paste the 12-row sector table (sector angle freq% A k v_free v_wake) and the power curve (wind_speed power, in W or kW — detected automatically). Optionally add a windPRO AEP reference for validation and a wind scale factor for the time-series method.

4

Set your months and check configuration

The Months in scope box defines your bat-active season — any subset of the 12 calendar months, in any order, German or English names. NZ windows, monthly thresholds, classic scheme and the temperature filter are pre-filled with the standard April–October defaults and fully editable.

5

Calculate & export

Results show full AEP, classic loss, NZ loss and the saving per turbine, plus the full NZ matrix. Export everything as a multi-sheet .xlsx. Results and exports are always in German.
